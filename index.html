Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about food safety, reservations, and NGO food donations.

Which locations are currently active on Cravely?
Cravely is currently active across major hubs including Bandra, Andheri, Ghatkopar, Grant Road, and Vasai, with new partner restaurants joining daily.
What information is required when reserving a meal?
When reserving a meal, we ask for your full name and mobile phone number so the restaurant can verify your order at the pickup counter and send you real-time SMS pickup reminders.
What happens if surplus food is still remaining at night?
Restaurants never have to throw away edible food! Cravely connects with verified NGOs (Roti Bank, Robin Hood Army, Feeding India). With one click, nearby volunteers pick up remaining portions and distribute them to local shelters.
How does Cravely ensure the food is safe and fresh?
All surplus meals listed on Cravely are fresh, perfectly edible food prepared on the same day by verified partner restaurants and bakeries. Items are listed only within certified safe consumption windows.
How do I pick up my reserved meal as a customer?
Once you reserve and pay via UPI, you receive an instant Order Pickup ID and QR code. Simply show this at the restaurant's takeaway counter during your scheduled pickup window.
Do restaurants get tax benefits for NGO donations?
Yes. All food donations logged through verified NGO partners on Cravely generate digital donation receipts eligible for tax exemption under Section 80G and corporate social responsibility (CSR) compliance.
